## History

The original Modern_ActiveDirectory project was created to provide a simple and practical way to assess the health of Active Directory environments.

Over time, the project gained visibility within the community and was adopted by administrators, consultants, and security professionals looking for a lightweight and accessible health check solution.

As usage increased, new requirements, improvement requests, and feature ideas emerged. Rather than continuing to extend the original codebase indefinitely, a new project was created to provide a cleaner foundation for future development.

ModernAD Community is the next step in that journey.

Built on the experience gained from the original project, ModernAD Community aims to improve maintainability, reliability, reporting capabilities, and overall user experience while preserving the original objective: helping organizations better understand and monitor the health of their Active Directory environments.

The original Modern_ActiveDirectory repository remains available as an archived project for historical reference.
